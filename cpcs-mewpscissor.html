Course Objective

The CPCS A25 MEWP Scissor training course is designed to equip participants with the theoretical knowledge and practical expertise required to safely operate scissor-type Mobile Elevating Work Platforms (MEWPs) in compliance with industry regulations.

This course focuses on safe operating procedures, hazard identification, emergency protocols, and effective maneuvering of scissor lifts in construction and industrial environments. Participants will gain essential skills for assessing worksite conditions, setting up equipment, and conducting operations at height while adhering to safety standards.

What You'll Learn

  • Understand the purpose, function, and types of scissor lifts used in various industries.
  • Recognize the responsibilities of an MEWP operator, including legal and safety obligations.
  • Identify key machine components, control functions, and safety features.
  • Conduct thorough pre-use inspections, safety checks, and emergency lowering function tests.
  • Understand and follow the manufacturer’s operational guidelines and relevant regulations.
  • Safely travel with a scissor lift across different surfaces, including inclines and confined spaces.
  • Position and stabilize the platform for various working heights and applications.
  • Operate the scissor lift effectively to access elevated work areas while maintaining safety compliance.
  • Assess environmental hazards, including overhead and underground obstructions.
  • Utilize extension decks safely and determine load distribution for stability.
  • Execute proper shutdown procedures and secure the equipment after use.
  • Follow correct procedures for loading, unloading, and transporting the MEWP scissor lift.

Assessment

The assessment process consists of both theoretical and practical evaluations. Candidates must complete a written examination covering safety protocols, operational techniques, and industry regulations.

Additionally, candidates will undergo a hands-on practical assessment, where they must demonstrate proficiency in pre-use inspections, safe maneuvering, platform positioning, and emergency response procedures.

Successful candidates will receive certification upon completing all assessment components to the required standard.

Certification

Upon successful completion of the course, participants will be awarded a Red CPCS Trained Operator Card for the A25 MEWP Scissor category. This certification is valid for two years and serves as proof of training and competency.

To upgrade to the Blue CPCS Competence Card, candidates must complete an NVQ Level 2 in Plant Operations within the two-year validity period. The Blue CPCS Card is recognized across the industry as a qualification for experienced MEWP scissor lift operators.

Course Summary

The A25 MEWP Scissor course is a structured training program that combines essential theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical instruction to ensure participants develop the necessary skills to operate scissor lifts safely and efficiently.

This course is suitable for individuals new to scissor lift operation as well as experienced operators seeking formal certification. It covers key aspects of machine controls, safety measures, hazard identification, and best practices for operating at height.
