Course Objective

The A42 Crusher CPCS course is designed to give operators the knowledge and practical skills to set up, operate and shut down a mobile crusher safely and efficiently on construction, demolition and recycling sites.

The course suits both novice operators and experienced operators who need formal CPCS certification, and focuses on safe working practices, material processing and working alongside loading machines.

What You'll Learn

  • The roles and responsibilities of a crusher operator, including health and safety regulations.
  • Identifying the main components of a crusher, including the feed hopper, jaw or impact chamber, conveyors and control systems.
  • Carrying out pre-use checks in line with the manufacturer's handbook.
  • Setting up the machine on site, including levelling and positioning for safe loading.
  • Feeding material safely and managing blockages using correct isolation procedures.
  • Establishing exclusion zones and working safely with loading plant and site personnel.
  • Preparing the crusher for travel and transport.
  • Shut-down, securing and end-of-shift procedures.

Assessment

Candidates complete the CPCS technical test, made up of a theory test and a practical test. The theory test covers safety, operating principles and site procedures. The practical test assesses your ability to set up, operate and shut down a crusher safely.

Certification

On successful completion of the course and CPCS technical test, you will receive the CPCS Red Trained Operator card, valid for 2 years. To progress to the Blue Competent Operator card, you will need to complete a relevant NVQ and demonstrate on-site experience. The Blue card is valid for five years and recognised across the UK.

Course Summary

This course combines classroom learning with hands-on practical training, delivered by experienced instructors. By the end of the course you will be ready to sit the CPCS technical tests and work as a crusher operator on sites across the UK.
