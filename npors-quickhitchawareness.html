N726 Quick Hitch Awareness Course

NPORS Quick Hitch Awareness training for operatives using quick hitch systems in construction and allied environments. Focused on safety and operational requirements.

Course Objective

This course is designed for operatives working with machinery equipped with quick hitch systems. Participants will learn the operational and safety requirements for using quick hitches effectively and safely in construction and related industries.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the industry, associated dangers, and responsibilities as a plant operator.
  • Gain familiarity with the manufacturer’s handbook for specific quick hitch systems.
  • Identify and explain the major components and functions of quick hitch devices.
  • Recognize and maintain appropriate PPE for quick hitch use.
  • Understand different types of quick hitch devices.
  • Perform pre-operational checks according to manufacturer and legislative requirements.
  • Conduct safety checks at designated change-over areas.
  • Fit and remove attachments safely and efficiently.
  • Demonstrate secure attachment checks.
  • Complete end-of-shift shutdown and parking procedures.

Assessment

Participants will undergo:

  • **NPORS Theory Assessment**: Open and multiple-choice questions.
  • **NPORS Practical Assessment**: Hands-on demonstration of skills.

Certification

Successful participants will receive the following certification options:

  • **NPORS Traditional Operator Card**: Ideal for industries outside the construction sector where a CSCS logo is not required. Valid for 3 or 5 years, depending on specific sector or company requirements.
  • **NPORS / CSCS Operator Card**: Required for the construction sector and follows the trained operator-to-competent operator card route, which involves NVQ achievement.

All applicants must pass the CITB Health, Safety & Environmental test within 2 years prior to certification application.

Course Summary

The NPORS Quick Hitch Awareness course equips operatives with the knowledge and skills to safely and effectively use quick hitch systems. This training ensures compliance with safety standards and promotes operational excellence across construction and allied environments.
