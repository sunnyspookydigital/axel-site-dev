Course Objective

This course is designed to equip operators with the essential skills and knowledge needed to safely and efficiently operate an agricultural tractor. By the end of the training, participants will have a strong understanding of industry standards, safety procedures, and operational best practices.

NPORS certification is highly valued across various industries, including construction, utilities, and agriculture, and provides operators with a widely recognized qualification.

What You'll Learn

  • An understanding of the agricultural plant operation industry and the key safety practices.
  • Knowledge of the roles and responsibilities of a plant operator.
  • Comprehensive safety training, focusing on hazard awareness and safe working procedures.
  • Familiarity with the latest legal regulations governing plant operations and safety standards.
  • Introduction to tractor controls, systems, and pre-shift safety checks.
  • Basic maintenance procedures, such as managing fuel, coolant, and lubrication.
  • Detailed instruction on tractor mechanisms and controls to ensure proper operation.
  • Techniques for loading, unloading, and maneuvering with trailers.
  • How to inspect and use mounted implements safely.
  • Practical hands-on sessions to reinforce skills and build confidence in operating agricultural tractors safely.

Assessment

The assessment includes a theory test (25 multiple-choice questions) that evaluates your knowledge of tractor operations and safety. In addition, there is a practical assessment where your hands-on proficiency will be tested.

Certification

Upon completion, participants can choose the NPORS-CSCS route and receive a Red Trained Operator card valid for 2 years. If they hold a relevant NVQ, they can upgrade to a Blue Competent Operator card, which is valid for 5 years. Those who do not pass the CITB Health, Safety & Environment test may receive a traditional 3-year NPORS card but without CSCS recognition.

Course Summary

This course provides a well-rounded combination of theoretical instruction and practical hands-on training, fully preparing you for the NPORS assessment. Additional study materials are provided to help participants who are new to Agricultural Tractor operation.

Led by experienced instructors, the course emphasizes practical skills and theoretical knowledge, ensuring that you leave with the confidence and competence to safely operate an agricultural tractor.
