N201 Excavator 180 Operator Course

Gain certification in wheeled loading shovel operation with Axel Construction Training's NPORS training. This course offers comprehensive theoretical and practical training on safe and effective machine operation.

01Course Objective

This course is ideal for operators of all skill levels, whether they are new to operating 180-degree excavators or experienced professionals. The training is aligned with NPORS standards, combining theoretical knowledge with practical application to ensure participants gain a thorough understanding of best practices and safety protocols in excavator operation.

02What You Will Learn

  • Understand the role and responsibilities of a plant operator within the industry.
  • Recognize the purpose, components, controls, and terminology associated with wheeled loading shovels.
  • Comply with manufacturer’s requirements, following the operator’s handbook and relevant regulations.
  • Conduct all essential pre-use checks on wheeled loading shovels.
  • Configure the plant for safe travel on both site and highways.
  • Operate on various terrains, including rough ground, inclines, and level surfaces, both with and without loads.
  • Safely maneuver in confined spaces.
  • Prepare the loading shovel for excavation, loading, and handling duties.
  • Identify and respond to hazards, including underground and overhead utilities.
  • Extract materials from stockpiles and create segregated storage areas.
  • Sort and place materials into transporting vehicles or hoppers.
  • Level ground and materials according to project requirements.
  • Apply safe shutdown and securing procedures for the equipment.
  • Understand loading and unloading procedures for safe machine transportation.
  • Carry out end-of-shift and shutdown procedures effectively.

03Assessment

The course includes an NPORS theory assessment with multiple-choice questions and a practical evaluation to ensure operators can perform all necessary tasks safely and competently.

04Certification

Traditional Card: The NPORS Traditional Card does not require an NVQ qualification. However, if the ‘HS&E TESTED Logo’ is desired, candidates must have passed the CITB Health, Safety, and Environment Test within the last two years. The card is valid for 3 or 5 years.

NPORS/CPCS (No NVQ): Operators who do not yet hold the relevant NVQ will receive a Trained Operator card valid for 2 years after testing. During this period, they must complete the required NVQ. Upon completion, a Competent Operator card, valid for 5 years, will be issued along with a CPD record/operator logbook.

NPORS/CPCS (NVQ Held): Operators already holding the requisite NVQ will receive a Competent Operator card valid for 5 years. They will also be issued a CPD record/operator logbook to document their professional development and working hours.

Proof of passing the CITB Health, Safety & Environment Test within the past two years is required for all applications.

NPORS Card Expiry Options: NPORS cards can feature individual expiry dates per category or a single expiration date for all categories held.

05Course Summary

This five-day course provides essential training for operating wheeled loading shovels. Through a blend of theory and practice, operators develop the skills needed for safe machine handling and workplace efficiency. Upon successful course completion, operators will be eligible for NPORS certification, valid for up to 5 years.
