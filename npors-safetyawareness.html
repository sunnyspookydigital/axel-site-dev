S001 Safety Awareness Course

Learn about the NPORS Safety Awareness (S001) course, covering key health and safety principles for construction site operatives, including Labourers Green Card eligibility.

Course Objective

The NPORS Construction Safety Awareness Course has been mapped as a full equivalent to the QCF Level 1 Award in Health and Safety in a Construction Environment. This course not only benefits plant operators but also enables labourers to apply for the CSCS Green Labourers Card upon passing the CITB Health, Safety, and Environment Operatives Test.

What You Will Learn

  • The importance of accident prevention on-site.
  • Basic understanding of health and safety laws.
  • How your role fits into the control and management of the site.
  • Risk assessments and the purpose of method statements.
  • Performing tasks safely and knowing when to ask for advice.
  • Reporting unsafe acts to help prevent potential accidents.

Assessment

The course includes an NPORS Theory Assessment consisting of open questions and multiple-choice tests to evaluate your understanding of the course material.

Certification

Labourers who successfully complete the NPORS Site Safety Awareness Course can apply for the CSCS Green Labourers Card, provided they also pass the CITB Health, Safety, and Environment Operatives Test. This ensures they meet the basic health and safety competency standards for construction site work.

Course Summary

  • Audience: Plant operators and labourers aiming to enhance safety awareness and secure CSCS Green Labourers Cards.
  • Recognised By: Endorsed by Build UK as standard training for all site operatives.
  • Industry Standards: Updated to reflect current legislation and the United Kingdom Contractors Group Health & Safety Training Standard.
