N132 Plant Mover Course

NPORS Plant Mover training for operators handling machinery in non-operational roles. Covers safety, machinery movement, and loading procedures.

Course Objective

This course is tailored for operators involved in the safe movement of plant machinery for non-operational duties, such as mechanics and delivery drivers. Participants will gain knowledge and skills to safely handle machinery for maintenance, repair, and transportation.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the industry, associated dangers, and operator responsibilities.
  • Familiarize with the manufacturer’s handbook for machinery use.
  • Locate and identify major machine components and controls, and explain their functions.
  • Perform pre-operational checks per manufacturer and legislative standards.
  • Identify and use appropriate PPE for plant mover operations.
  • Prepare and operate machinery safely and efficiently.
  • Conduct safety checks at the work area and consider environmental factors.
  • Learn procedures for storing and transporting machinery attachments.
  • Understand safe loading and unloading processes for machine transportation.
  • Execute end-of-shift shutdown and parking procedures.

Assessment

Participants will undergo:

  • NPORS Theory Assessment: Open and multiple-choice questions.
  • NPORS Practical Assessment: Hands-on evaluation of skills.

Certification

Successful participants will receive one of the following NPORS cards:

  • Trained Operator Card (RED): Valid for 2 years for operators without an NVQ. NVQ completion within this timeframe is required to upgrade.
  • Competent Operator Card (BLUE): Valid for 5 years for operators with the requisite NVQ, along with a CPD logbook for ongoing professional development.

All applicants must pass the CITB Health, Safety & Environmental test within 2 years prior to applying for certification.

Course Summary

The NPORS Plant Mover course provides essential training for operators who handle machinery in non-operational roles. This includes plant mechanics, delivery drivers, and other personnel responsible for machinery movement. The course emphasizes safety, maintenance, and efficient machinery handling.
