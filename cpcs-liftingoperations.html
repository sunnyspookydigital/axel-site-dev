Course Objective

This course is specifically designed to equip participants with the skills and knowledge required to effectively plan and manage lifting operations in accordance with CPCS standards. It ensures participants understand legislative requirements and industry best practices, enabling them to take on the responsibilities of an appointed person.

Participants will gain practical experience in lifting operations planning, including risk assessments, method statements, and operational strategies to enhance site safety and efficiency.

What You'll Learn

  • Comprehensive understanding of legal frameworks and regulations governing lifting operations.
  • Roles and responsibilities of personnel involved in lifting operations, including the appointed person, lifting supervisor, and others.
  • Identification and application of various types of lifting equipment and their capabilities and limitations.
  • Detailed knowledge of lifting accessories, including inspections, maintenance, and capacity calculations.
  • Planning and executing basic, standard, and complex lifting operations safely and efficiently.
  • Managing site-specific hazards and ensuring compliance with safety protocols for lifting operations.
  • Understanding load weight calculations, lifting angles, and center of gravity considerations for safe lifting practices.
  • Practical application of lift planning, including communication, coordination, and supervision techniques.

Assessment

Participants will undergo both theoretical instruction and practical exercises. The course concludes with a CPCS technical test that assesses understanding and competency in planning and managing lifting operations.

Assessments include written exams, practical demonstrations, and scenario-based planning exercises to evaluate proficiency in real-world lifting operations.

Certification

Upon successful completion, participants will receive the CPCS Red Trained Operator Card, valid for two years. During this time, they can achieve an NVQ Level 5 Diploma, which leads to the CPCS Blue Competence Card, renewable every five years.

This certification demonstrates the ability to safely and efficiently manage lifting operations, ensuring compliance with industry regulations and safety standards.

Course Summary

The CPCS Appointed Person (A61) Course provides a robust foundation in the legal, technical, and practical aspects of managing lifting operations. It combines detailed theoretical instruction with hands-on training to ensure participants are fully prepared to take on the responsibilities of an appointed person.

The course is designed for individuals who oversee lifting operations, including site managers, supervisors, and crane coordinators, providing them with the skills needed to maintain a safe and compliant lifting environment.
