N133 Plant Machinery Marshal Course

NPORS-certified training for Plant Machinery Marshals. Covers legislation, safety procedures, communication, and guiding machinery efficiently.

01Course Objective

This course equips participants with the necessary skills and knowledge to operate as a Plant Machinery Marshal safely and effectively. The training covers legislation, safety checks, communication methods, and guiding machinery in various environments.

02What You Will Learn

  • Understand the industry, relevant legislation, and operator responsibilities.
  • Familiarize with the manufacturer’s handbook for guided machinery.
  • Learn approved hand signals, signs, and communication methods for efficient guidance.
  • Identify and maintain appropriate PPE for machinery marshals.
  • Perform safety checks, including stop blocks and tipping areas.
  • Establish exclusion zones for loading and unloading activities.
  • Guide machinery safely in forward, reverse, confined spaces, and “blind areas.”
  • Understand environmental considerations during operations.
  • Carry out end-of-shift procedures, including shutdown and parking.

03Assessment

Participants will be evaluated through:

  • **NPORS Theory Assessment**: Open questions and multiple choice.
  • **NPORS Practical Assessment**: Hands-on evaluation of skills.

04Certification

Successful participants will be awarded one of the following NPORS cards:

  • Trained Operator Card (RED): Valid for 2 years for those without an NVQ. NVQ completion within this period is required to upgrade.
  • Competent Operator Card (BLUE): Valid for 5 years for those with the requisite NVQ, including a CPD logbook for continuous professional development.

All applicants must pass the CITB Health, Safety & Environmental test within 2 years prior to card application.

05Course Summary

The NPORS Plant Machinery Marshal course is designed for individuals responsible for guiding and overseeing the safe operation of plant machinery. It ensures adherence to industry safety standards and provides comprehensive training in efficient machinery guidance and communication.
