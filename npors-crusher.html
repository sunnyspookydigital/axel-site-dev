N207 Crusher Operation Course

Learn to operate crushers safely and efficiently with Axel Construction Training's NPORS assessment. This course offers hands-on training in safe crusher operation, pre-operational checks, and environmental considerations.

Course Objective

This course is suitable for operators of all skill levels. Whether you are an experienced operator or new to crusher operations, this course is designed to provide comprehensive training based on NPORS learning outcomes. It combines both theoretical knowledge and practical tuition to ensure a thorough understanding of safe crusher operation and best practices.

What You Will Learn

  • Understanding the plant operation industry and its inherent risks.
  • Working knowledge of the manufacturer’s manual for the specific machine.
  • Identifying and explaining the functions of major crusher components.
  • Using remote control systems to operate crushers safely.
  • Performing pre-operational checks based on manufacturer and legal requirements.
  • Ensuring proper use and maintenance of appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
  • Correct mounting and dismounting techniques for the crusher.
  • Starting and stopping the engine, belts, feeder, and jaws/hammer safely.
  • Setting up the machine for travel and operating it across various terrains.
  • Conducting safety checks on the worksite before starting the crushing tasks.
  • Understanding different types of crushers and their advantages, disadvantages, and usage.
  • Carrying out emergency stops and applying safety precautions during operations.
  • Performing crushing tasks with a focus on safety and efficiency.
  • Knowledge of loading and unloading procedures for crusher transportation.
  • Considering environmental factors during operation.
  • Following end-of-shift procedures and safely shutting down the equipment.

Assessment

The assessment will consist of both theoretical and practical components. The theory test includes multiple-choice questions, while the practical assessment involves demonstrating safe and effective crusher operation in a work environment.

Certification

Upon successful completion, participants will be eligible for the NPORS Traditional Card. This card does not require an NVQ qualification, but applicants must have passed the CITB Health, Safety, and Environment Test within two years if they want the ‘HS&E TESTED’ logo on the card. The NPORS card is valid for 3 or 5 years.

New entrants who do not hold the necessary NVQ qualification will receive a Trained Operator card, valid for 2 years. During this time, they will need to complete the NVQ requirements to upgrade to a Competent Operator card, which will be valid for 5 years.

Operators who already possess the required NVQ and pass the assessment will be issued a Competent Operator card valid for 5 years.

All applicants must provide proof of passing the CITB Health, Safety, and Environment Test within the last two years at the time of application.

NPORS cards can be issued with an individual expiry date for each category or a unified expiry date for all categories.

Course Summary

This course, which can be completed in one to three days depending on individual requirements, provides participants with the knowledge and skills to operate crushers safely and efficiently. It combines theory with practical experience, ensuring a well-rounded understanding of all aspects of crusher operation.

After completing the course and passing the assessment, participants will receive an NPORS certification, demonstrating their ability to work safely and competently as a crusher operator.
