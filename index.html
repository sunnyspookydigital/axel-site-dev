FAQ

Questions, answered

What's the difference between CPCS and NPORS?

Both are recognised plant operator schemes. CPCS gives crucial accreditations for plant operators, guaranteeing compliance and site safety. NPORS, established in 1992, is a highly adaptable and respected route for securing your plant machinery ticket across Construction, Industrial, Warehousing and Marine sectors. We train and assess for both, so we can advise which suits your job.

How long is a CPCS card valid?

After passing your CPCS tests you receive the Red Trained Operator card, valid for 2 years. To progress to the Blue Competent Operator card you complete a relevant NVQ and demonstrate on-site experience. The Blue card is valid for five years and recognised across the UK.

Which NPORS card will I get?

The NPORS Traditional card does not require an NVQ and is valid for 3 or 5 years. With the NPORS/CPCS card, operators without the NVQ receive a Trained Operator card valid for 2 years, and operators who hold the NVQ receive a Competent Operator card valid for 5 years, plus a CPD logbook.

Do I need the CITB Health, Safety & Environment test?

Yes. Proof of passing the CITB Health, Safety & Environment Test within the last two years is required for certification applications, and you need a valid CITB HS&E certificate to enrol on an NVQ assessment.

Can I train around my current job?

Yes. We offer weekend and evening options so you can secure your qualifications without disrupting your current job.

How long does a course take?

It depends on the machine and your experience. For example, the A02 Crawler Crane and N202 Excavator 360 courses run over 10 days, while experienced operators can often go straight to a shorter test. Tell us your experience and we'll recommend the quickest route.

Do you offer group bookings?

Yes. We offer NVQ assessments at competitive rates, with discounts for group registrations. Get in touch with the number of people and the categories you need.
