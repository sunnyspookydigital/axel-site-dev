N209 Loading Shovel Course

Enquire about this course07454 623678

Course Objective

This course provides comprehensive training for safe and efficient operation of a loading shovel. It includes tailored training durations for learners with varying levels of experience, from beginners to experienced operators requiring assessment.

What You Will Learn

  • Understanding the industry, potential hazards, and operator responsibilities.
  • Familiarity with the manufacturer's handbook for the specific loading shovel being used.
  • Identifying major components of the loading shovel and their functions.
  • Understanding key controls and their functions for efficient operation.
  • Conducting pre-operational checks in compliance with manufacturer and legislative requirements.
  • Identifying and maintaining PPE appropriate for loading shovel use.
  • Performing safety checks at the work area before operation.
  • Preparing and operating the loading shovel safely and efficiently.
  • Understanding environmental considerations during operation.
  • Proper storage and transportation of implements and attachments.
  • Demonstrating knowledge of loading and unloading procedures for machine transportation.
  • Completing end-of-shift and shutdown procedures effectively.

Assessment

Participants will undergo a theory assessment, comprising open and multiple-choice questions, and a practical assessment to demonstrate their competency in operating the loading shovel.

Certification

Existing NPORS Card Holder – No (S)NVQ Held: A Trained Operator card valid for 2 years is issued to operators who do not hold the requisite (S)NVQ. During this period, operators must achieve the required (S)NVQ for further certification.

Existing NPORS Card Holder – (S)NVQ Held: A Competent Operator card valid for 5 years is issued to operators holding the requisite (S)NVQ. These operators also receive a CPD record/operator logbook to document their professional development and operational hours.

Course Summary

The N209 Loading Shovel Training equips participants with the necessary knowledge and practical skills to operate a loading shovel safely. This course is suitable for operators across various experience levels, focusing on safety, compliance, and operational efficiency to meet NPORS standards.
