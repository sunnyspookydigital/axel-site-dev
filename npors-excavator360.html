N202 Excavator 360 Operator Course

Enquire about this course07454 623678

Course Objective

This course aims to provide operators with the knowledge and skills needed to safely and effectively operate an Excavator 360. Designed to meet NPORS standards, the training combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on practice, ensuring participants gain a solid understanding of best practices, safety protocols, and machine handling techniques.

What You Will Learn

  • Develop awareness of industry hazards and the responsibilities of a plant operator.
  • Gain familiarity with the manufacturer's handbook for the excavator model being used.
  • Identify major components of the machine and understand their functions.
  • Locate and understand the purpose of steering, driving, and braking controls.
  • Choose and maintain appropriate PPE for excavator use.
  • Perform all pre-operational checks according to manufacturer and legal guidelines.
  • Safely mount and dismount the excavator.
  • Start, stop, and maneuver the machine safely, adapting to different terrains and confined spaces.
  • Conduct site safety checks before beginning work.
  • Master excavation techniques for various scenarios.
  • Load materials into transport vehicles or storage containers safely and efficiently.
  • Restore excavated areas by grading and leveling to original contours.
  • Attach and remove equipment attachments as required.
  • Complete end-of-shift and shutdown procedures.

Assessment

The course includes an NPORS theory test with multiple-choice questions and a practical assessment to verify each operator’s competency in real-world settings.

Certification

Traditional Card: The NPORS Traditional Card does not require an NVQ qualification. If the ‘HS&E TESTED Logo’ is required, candidates must pass the CITB Health, Safety, and Environment Test within two years of application. This card is valid for either 3 or 5 years.

NPORS/CPCS (No NVQ): Operators without the NVQ will receive a Trained Operator card valid for 2 years. During this period, they must complete the NVQ. Upon completion, they are eligible for a Competent Operator card, valid for 5 years, and will also receive a CPD logbook.

NPORS/CPCS (NVQ Held): Operators with the NVQ will receive a Competent Operator card, valid for 5 years. They will also receive a CPD logbook to record their professional development and work hours.

Proof of passing the CITB Health, Safety & Environment Test within the last two years is required for all certification applications.

NPORS Card Expiry Options: NPORS cards can have separate expiry dates for each category, or a single expiry for all held categories.

Course Summary

This comprehensive 10-day course provides essential training for safe and effective operation of Excavator 360 machinery. Combining theoretical and practical elements, the course ensures operators are fully equipped to handle real-world challenges in construction and related industries. Participants will gain expertise in operating the machine across various terrains, performing excavation tasks, and adhering to workplace safety standards.

By the end of the course, participants will have developed critical skills, including pre-operational checks, maneuvering in confined spaces, and efficiently loading materials. The program also emphasizes the importance of professional conduct and continuous development through a structured CPD logbook system. Upon successful completion, participants will receive NPORS certification, valid for up to 5 years, affirming their competency as Excavator 360 operators.
