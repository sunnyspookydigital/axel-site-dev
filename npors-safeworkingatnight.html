N404 Safe Working at Height

Learn safe working practices at height with our NPORS N404 Safe Working at Height course. Practical and theory-based training to ensure safety and compliance.

Course Objective

The NPORS N404 Safe Working at Height course provides candidates with the practical and theoretical knowledge required to safely perform tasks at height, ensuring compliance with industry standards and legislative requirements.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the dangers of working in the industry and the responsibilities of an operator.
  • Gain knowledge of the manufacturer’s handbook and relevant legislation.
  • Locate and identify the major components of the machinery and explain their functions.
  • Perform pre-operational checks in accordance with manufacturer’s and legislative requirements.
  • Identify and maintain appropriate PPE for tasks involving petrol-driven cut-off saws.
  • Select suitable types of abrasive wheels and battery/petrol cut-off saws for specific tasks.
  • Conduct necessary safety checks at the worksite and establish safety zones.
  • Prepare, operate, and manage machinery safely and efficiently.
  • Understand environmental considerations for operations.
  • Learn best practices for storing and transporting abrasive wheels and battery/petrol cut-off saws.
  • Perform end-of-shift and shutdown procedures safely and correctly.

Assessment

To achieve the NPORS Operator Card, candidates must pass both the NPORS Theory Test and Practical Test.

Certification

Upon successful completion, candidates will receive the NPORS Operator Card, valid for three years. This certification is recognised nationally and internationally across various industry sectors.

Course Summary

  • Duration: 0.5 days.
  • Course Ratios: 6 delegates per session, 1 machine, 1 day for novices.
  • Course Aims: To provide candidates with thorough practical and theoretical training in the safe operation of handheld abrasive wheel cut-off saws.
