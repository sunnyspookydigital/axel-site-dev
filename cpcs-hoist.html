01Course Objective

The A20 Hoist course is designed to provide participants with the skills and knowledge required to safely operate a hoist. Whether you are new to hoist operation or seeking to formalize your experience, this course ensures you gain the necessary qualifications to work safely and effectively on construction sites.

This course includes a thorough analysis of hoist operations, safety checks, maintenance procedures, and emergency protocols. Through both theoretical and practical training, candidates will gain competency in hoist operation, ensuring safety and operational efficiency.

02What You'll Learn

  • Understand the nature of the hoist sector and the responsibilities of an operator in maintaining site safety.
  • Identify various types of hoists, their components, functions, and key terminologies.
  • Interpret and follow manufacturer guidelines, manuals, and relevant safety regulations.
  • Assess hoist limitations and ensure all pre-use checks are thoroughly conducted before operation.
  • Inspect gates, safety devices, and hoist functionality during running checks to ensure readiness.
  • Ensure hoist stability, system alignment, and effective hoist-way protection for safe operation.
  • Calculate hoist capacity and verify that it can safely accommodate required loads or passengers.
  • Execute emergency lowering and evacuation procedures in compliance with safety protocols (Endorsements B & D).
  • Safely prepare the hoist for various load types, ensuring suitability for transport.
  • Operate the hoist to its full travel range and across different landing levels, both loaded and unloaded, in a controlled and efficient manner.
  • Communicate clearly with colleagues and follow site safety procedures through signals, instructions, and protocols.
  • Safely secure the hoist, placing it in an out-of-service condition and isolating it after use.

03Assessment

The A20 Hoist course includes both classroom-based theoretical instruction and practical exercises. After the training, candidates must complete a one-day assessment, which includes technical tests that cover hoist operation, safety checks, and emergency procedures.

The practical assessment requires candidates to demonstrate their competency in operating a hoist safely, while the theoretical test ensures they understand key safety regulations and operational protocols.

04Certification

Upon successful completion of the technical tests, learners will receive a Red CPCS Trained Operator Card, valid for two years. This card confirms the participant's ability to safely operate a hoist in compliance with industry regulations.

To advance their career and move to a Blue CPCS Competence Card, participants must complete an NVQ Level 2 in Plant Operations within the two-year validity period of their Red Card. The Blue Card demonstrates full occupational competence and enhances employability within the construction industry.

05Course Summary

The A20 Hoist course offers a comprehensive training experience that combines classroom learning with hands-on practical sessions. This ensures participants are well-prepared to operate hoists safely and efficiently on construction sites.

The course is suitable for both new operators and those seeking to formalize their existing experience with a recognized qualification. Participants will gain the skills needed to meet industry standards and ensure a safe working environment.
